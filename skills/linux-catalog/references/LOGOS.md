# Logo sources and licenses

Logos are stored locally in `apps/web/public/logos/`. All marks are used for identification; each logo, product name, and trademark remains the property of its respective owner. Local copies are not endorsement.

Package logos: 90 from Simple Icons, 2 from Devicon, and 68 initials fallbacks (the catalog has 160 applications). The five distribution marks are from Simple Icons.

Simple Icons is released under CC0, but it explicitly warns that this does not mean each included brand mark is CC0. The per-icon license is recorded below where Simple Icons provides one; otherwise the source/guidelines link is provided and the owner’s terms are unspecified by the icon set. Devicon’s collection is MIT; its README states product marks belong to their owners. See [Simple Icons disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md) and [Devicon repository](https://github.com/devicons/devicon).
